How to Balance College and a Startup

We interviewed 20 student founders on how they managed attendance, exams, and fundraising.

Building a company while pursuing a degree is one of the hardest challenges a student can face. Yet, some of the world's biggest companies (Facebook, Google, Microsoft) started in dorm rooms. Here is how successful student founders manage the chaos.

1. The 80/20 Attendance Rule

"I knew exactly how many classes I could miss," says Karthik, a founder from Chennai. "I maintained 75% attendance strictly and used the remaining 25% for client meetings. Don't aim for 100% if you're building a business."

2. Integrate Projects with Curriculum

Whenever a professor assigned a project, smart founders used it to build a feature for their startup. This kills two birds with one stone—you get grades and product development simultaneously.

3. Build a Complementary Team

If you have exams, your co-founder should be free. Don't pick co-founders who have the exact same exam schedule if possible, or plan your sprints around the academic calendar.
